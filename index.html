Questions

Is it free to join?

Yes. Join the Discord, post your in-game name, and you're in.

Do I have to be good at PvP?

No. Plenty of members mostly build. If you want to get better at PvP, we practice together.

Which server do you play on?

DonutSMP, at donutsmp.net. Our own server is on the way.

Do you sell items or money?

No. We never buy or sell DonutSMP items, money or accounts, and we never will.

How do I find you in game?

Ask in the Discord and someone will meet you, or look for our map art around the server.
