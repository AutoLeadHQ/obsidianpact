Privacy & safety

Short version: this is a plain information site for a Minecraft clan. It can't hurt your device and it doesn't collect anything personal.

What this site never does

  • Ask you to sign in, or ask for any password, code or account (Discord, Minecraft, Microsoft or anything else).
  • Download files to your device or ask you to install anything.
  • Show ads, use tracking cookies, or sell or share information about you.
  • Buy or sell DonutSMP items, money or accounts.

Where the links go

Every Join button goes straight to discord.gg/c9HDNeTX4z, Discord's own invite page. Videos play from YouTube or TikTok. Skin pictures on the members page come from mc-heads.net, using each member's public Minecraft name. If you'd rather not click anything, open Discord and join with the code c9HDNeTX4z.

Visit counts

To see how many people visit and which pages they like, the site keeps simple anonymous counts: which pages were viewed, roughly which country and city the visit came from (worked out from your connection, and the address itself is never saved), the kind of device and browser, which site sent you here, and whether you clicked Join. A random ID in your browser's storage tells a new visitor from a returning one. No names, no accounts, no cookies.

You can clear it any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The members page

You only appear on the members page if you set your IGN with Pact Bot in our Discord. It shows your IGN, your Discord picture, your clan role, when you joined and your About me. Want to be taken off? Ask a Leader in the Discord.

Check it yourself

All of this site's code is public at github.com/AutoLeadHQ/obsidianpact, so anyone can read exactly what it does.
