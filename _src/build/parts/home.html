Our server is live

The Obsidian Pact SMP is a Vanilla+ survival server: real survival, no pay-to-win, plus the quality-of-life stuff that makes an SMP fun. PvP, raiding and griefing are on.

How to join

  1. Open Minecraft Java Edition and click Multiplayer.
  2. Click Add Server and type obsidianpact.net as the address.
  3. Click Done, then join. Press Esc in game and click ⚔ Pact Menu to see everything.

Questions

Is this website safe?

Yes. It's a plain information page: no downloads, no sign-in, no ads, and it never asks for a password. The Join buttons go directly to discord.gg. If you'd rather skip the site entirely, open Discord and join with the code c9HDNeTX4z.

Is it free to join?

Yes. Join the Discord, set your IGN, and you're in.

Do I have to be good at PvP?

No. Plenty of members mostly build. If you want to get better at PvP, we practice together.

Which server do you play on?

Our own server, at obsidianpact.net. The clan started on DonutSMP (donutsmp.net) and still plays there too.

Is the server free?

Yes, it's free to join and play. There's an optional Obsidian+ rank with cosmetic perks and more homes, and nothing that gives anyone a fighting edge.

Do you sell items or money?

No. We never buy or sell DonutSMP items, money or accounts, and we never will.

How do I find you in game?

Ask in the Discord and someone will meet you, or look for our map art around the server.
